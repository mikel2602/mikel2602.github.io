Support

Answers to the questions we hear most often. If yours is not here, write to us.

How do I restore a purchase on a new device?

Sign in with the same Apple Account you used for the purchase, open the app and tap Restore Purchases. You will find it on the upgrade screen or in the app’s settings. Purchases belong to your Apple Account, not to a single device, so you can restore them whenever you need to.

How do I cancel a subscription?

On your iPhone or iPad, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Choose the subscription and tap Cancel Subscription. To avoid the next charge, cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Can I get a refund?

Apple processes every payment, so refund requests are decided by Apple too. You can ask for one at reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot issue refunds ourselves, because we never receive your payment.

Do I need an account?

No. Our apps do not ask you to sign up or log in, and they do not send what you create to us.

Something is not working. What should I try first?

Make sure the app and iOS are up to date, then close the app completely and open it again. If the problem continues, write to us with the details below and we will look into it.

Still need help?

Tell us which app you are using, your device and iOS version, and what happened. We read every message and reply as soon as we can.
