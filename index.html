Independent app development

Apps for iPhone and iPad, built by an independent developer: from the first idea to the release on the App Store.

This is the home of a small, independent mobile development practice. The work covers the whole life of an app: interface design, native development, testing on real devices, submission to App Review, and the updates that follow a release.

What we do

iOS development

Native apps for current versions of iPhone and iPad.

Media and creativity

Photos, audio and video handled on the device itself, without uploading.

Release and support

App Store submission, purchases through Apple, and maintenance after launch.

Privacy by default

The applications published here are built to work without accounts and without servers wherever that is possible. When an app does not need to send your material anywhere, it does not send it anywhere: photos, recordings and files stay on your device.
