Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

This policy describes how the iOS applications we publish on the App Store treat your information. It covers every one of those applications and this website.

In short: our applications do not collect your personal data.

There are no user accounts, no registration and no servers of our own. Your photos, videos, audio, files, progress and usage never leave your device unless you export or share them yourself.

1. Information we collect

None. We do not collect, store, transmit or process personal information. We never ask for your name, e-mail address, phone number, date of birth or location, there is nothing to register for, and we run no server that could receive data from an app.

In Apple's App Privacy questionnaire our applications are listed as Data Not Collected.

2. What the app uses on your device

Depending on its purpose, an application may ask iOS for permission to use one of the features below. It asks only for what it needs, at the moment you use the function that needs it. Whatever it reads is used on your device, for that function alone, and is not sent to us or to anyone else.

Camera

Used only when you choose to take a photo or record a video inside the app. The result is processed and kept in the app's own storage area on your device and is not uploaded.

Microphone

Used only by an app that records audio, and only while you are recording. Recordings are processed on your device and are not uploaded.

Files you choose

When an app can open a file of yours, you pick it yourself in the iOS file picker. The app reads only that file, on your device, and does not copy it to us or share it with anyone.

Photos

When you use a picture or video from your library, you select it in the iOS photo picker and the app receives only the items you chose. An app that saves what you make asks for permission to add items to your library and uses it only for the exports you request. The apps never browse or send your existing photos.

Settings and progress stored on the device

An app stores a few small items locally: your preferences, your progress, whether you have seen the introduction, and counters such as the number of free uses left. They live in the app's own storage, contain no personal information and never leave your device. Deleting the app removes them.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not use analytics, crash reporting or measurement tools.
  • We do not include advertising, advertising networks or the advertising identifier.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not build user profiles and do not use automated decision-making or profiling.
  • We do not sell, rent or share personal data, because we have none.
  • We do not embed third-party software development kits that collect data.

4. Subscriptions and payments

Some features are unlocked by an auto-renewing subscription or a one-time purchase. Every purchase is made and processed by Apple through the App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never see your card number, billing address or any other payment detail.

To know whether paid features should be unlocked, the app asks Apple's purchase system on the device whether a valid purchase or subscription exists. The reply says only whether the purchase is valid; it does not identify you to us. Apple's handling of purchases is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. Information Apple provides to us

As an App Store developer we receive aggregate statistics from Apple, such as the number of downloads or subscriptions per country. These figures are grouped and anonymous and cannot identify an individual user. We receive nothing else.

6. Children

Our applications are not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included. Since no personal data is collected at all, no information about a child can reach us.

7. Your rights and your control

Rights of access, correction, deletion, portability and objection, under the GDPR, the CCPA and comparable laws, concern data a company holds about you. We hold none, so there is nothing for us to disclose, correct, export or delete.

What is on your device stays under your control:

  • Camera, microphone and photo permissions can be withdrawn at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security on your device, or in the app's own entry in Settings.
  • Content and progress saved inside an app can be deleted from within the app where it offers that option.
  • Deleting the app removes everything it has stored on your device.
  • Items you have exported to Photos remain in your library until you delete them there.

8. Data retention and security

We keep no copies of your content, because none is ever sent to us. Photos, recordings, projects and progress are held in the app's private storage on your device, protected by iOS and by your passcode or biometric lock. We recommend keeping your device up to date and using a passcode.

9. Links to other services

An app may link to the App Store or to our support e-mail address. Those services are run by Apple or by your own mail provider, and their privacy terms apply once you leave the app.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date at the top. Important changes will also be mentioned in the release notes of the relevant update. Our applications link to this page, so you always see the current version.

11. Contact

For any question about this policy or about privacy in our applications, write to eesdemir.se@gmail.com. We reply to privacy questions as quickly as we can, and within 30 days at the latest.
