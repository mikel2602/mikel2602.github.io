Who we are and what this covers

This policy is published by the independent developer of the apps offered under this developer account on the App Store (“we”, “us”). The seller name shown on each app’s App Store page identifies us.

It covers every one of those apps, on iPhone, iPad and any other Apple device they run on, as well as this website. If a future version of an app ever needs to handle data in a way this page does not describe, we will update this policy before that version is released.

Personal data we collect

We do not collect personal data through our apps.

The apps have no sign-up, no login and no server of ours behind them. They do not send us your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, recordings, files, usage history or any identifier of your device. We keep no database of users, because there is nothing to put in one.

For the same reason, the App Privacy section of each of our apps on the App Store reads Data Not Collected.

Device features and permissions

Some apps need a protected feature of your device to do what you ask. iOS always asks for your permission first, and you are free to say no. If you allow access, the app uses it on the device, only for the function you are using at that moment, and never sends the result to us or to anyone else.

Camera

To take a photo or record a video inside the app. What you capture is kept in the app’s own storage, or saved where you choose.

Microphone

To record sound, for example as part of a video. The audio is processed on the device and is not uploaded.

Photos

To pick an image or video from your library, or to save something you made. With the iOS photo picker, the app only receives the items you select.

Files

To open a document or audio file you choose in the Files picker, or to export one. The app reads only the file you pick.

Any other feature that iOS protects with a permission prompt follows the same rule. You can review or withdraw a permission at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security, or on the app’s own page in Settings.

What is stored on your device

An app keeps a small amount of information locally so that it works the way you left it: settings, progress, saved projects, and simple counters such as how many free uses remain. This information stays in the app’s private storage, protected by iOS and by your passcode. We cannot see or reach it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Your device backups and, where an app offers it, a copy in your own iCloud account are stored by Apple under your Apple Account and governed by Apple’s terms. They are not available to us either.

Purchases and subscriptions

Paid features are sold through the App Store, as one-time in-app purchases or as auto-renewable subscriptions. Apple processes the payment from start to finish. We never receive your card details, your billing address or the details of your Apple Account.

To decide whether to unlock paid features, the app asks Apple’s purchase system on your device whether a valid purchase exists. That check confirms the purchase, not who you are, and its result is not sent to us. How Apple handles your purchase is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.

Information Apple shares with developers

Apple gives every developer some information about how their apps are doing. For us, this is limited to:

  • aggregated figures such as downloads, sales, subscriptions and the countries they come from, which do not identify anyone;
  • crash reports and diagnostics, only if you have chosen to share them with app developers in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements. These reports are anonymous, and we use them only to fix problems;
  • ratings and reviews you publish on the App Store, which are public by nature.

We do not combine this information with anything else, and we do not try to identify anyone from it.

No advertising, tracking or data-collecting SDKs

  • No analytics or measurement tools are built into our apps.
  • No advertising networks and no use of the advertising identifier. Because the apps do not track you, they never need to show the App Tracking Transparency prompt.
  • No fingerprinting, profiling or automated decision-making about you.
  • No social login and no third-party software kits that collect data.
  • We do not sell or share personal data. We hold none to sell or share.

When you email us

If you write to us, we receive your email address, your name if your mail program adds it, and whatever you choose to put in the message. We use this only to answer you and to fix the problem you describe. We never add you to a mailing list.

Our mailbox is provided by Google (Gmail), so messages are also processed under the Google Privacy Policy. We delete correspondence once it is no longer needed to help you, and you can ask us to delete it sooner at any time. Where the GDPR applies, the legal basis for this is our legitimate interest in replying to the message you sent us.

This website

This site consists of static pages. It sets no cookies and uses no analytics, no forms, and no fonts or scripts loaded from other services.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. As the hosting provider, GitHub may record technical information such as visitors’ IP addresses in its server logs, for security and to keep the service running. This is described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement. We have no access to those logs.

Children

Our apps do not collect personal data from anyone, children included, so no information about a child can reach us through them. If a child has sent us an email and a parent or guardian would like it deleted, write to us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation, the California Consumer Privacy Act and similar laws in other countries give you the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or transfer your personal data, and to object to how it is used.

Because our apps collect no personal data, these rights in practice concern only emails you have sent us. We will act on any request about them within 30 days. You also have the right to complain to the data protection authority in the country where you live.

On your own device, you stay in control at all times:

  • Withdraw camera, microphone, photo or other permissions in Settings › Privacy & Security.
  • Delete saved content from within the app, where the app offers it.
  • Delete the app to remove everything it has stored on the device.

Security and retention

The safest data is data that is never collected, which is why our apps are designed not to transmit your material. What they store on your device is protected by iOS data protection, and we recommend keeping a passcode enabled and your software up to date. We retain no copies of your content. Emails are kept only as described above.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version on this page and update the effective date at the top. A change that affects what an app does with data will be published here before the app update that introduces it, and mentioned in that update’s release notes.
