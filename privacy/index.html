Privacy Policy

This policy applies to all apps published under this developer account on the App Store.

  • Effective 28 October 2026

Data we collect

Our apps do not collect, store or share personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics and no advertising.

Data on your device

If an app asks for access to a device feature such as the camera, microphone or photos, it uses that access only on your device, for the function you choose. Nothing is sent to us. You can change permissions at any time in your device settings, and deleting an app removes the data it stored.

Purchases

In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment information. See the Apple Privacy Policy.

Third parties

We do not use third-party analytics, advertising or tracking services, and we do not sell or share personal data. Apple may provide us with anonymous, aggregated app statistics, which do not identify you.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Retention and deletion

Because we collect no personal data, we hold none to retain. If you email us, we use your message only to reply and will delete it at your request.

Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always published on this page.
